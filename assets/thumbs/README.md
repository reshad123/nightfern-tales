# Thumbnail art (optional, but it makes the thumbnails much richer)

Put one background picture per story here, named after the story's id:

    assets/thumbs/night-train.jpg
    assets/thumbs/seaside-village.jpg
    assets/thumbs/lighthouse-night.jpg
    assets/thumbs/farm-night.jpg
    assets/thumbs/rainy-bookshop.jpg
    assets/thumbs/country-inn.jpg
    assets/thumbs/canal-boat.jpg
    assets/thumbs/night-garden.jpg
    assets/thumbs/forest-cabin.jpg
    assets/thumbs/moonlit-lake.jpg

16:9 pictures (1920x1080 is perfect). .jpg, .png or .webp all work. The pipeline adds the
title, the "SLEEP STORY" label and the length itself, so the pictures must have **no text**.
If a story has no picture here, a night scene is made from the video footage instead.

Keep the left third of each picture calmer and darker: that is where the title goes.

## Prompts (paste one into your image tool)

Common style, added to every prompt:
> Cinematic painterly digital illustration, 16:9, deep navy and teal night sky, a bright full
> moon, soft glowing stars, warm golden lamplight, gentle mist, magical but calm and cozy,
> rich detail, soft depth of field. No text, no letters, no watermark, no logo. Keep the left
> third of the image calm and dark with little detail.

Scene, one per story:

- **night-train**: A vintage red sleeper train curving along a snowy mountain ledge at night, warm golden windows glowing, snow-covered pine forest, jagged snowy peaks under a full moon.
- **seaside-village**: A tiny seaside village at night, stone cottages with glowing windows, fishing boats in a moonlit harbour, calm silver water, a lantern-lit path along the shore.
- **lighthouse-night**: A white lighthouse on a rocky coast under a sky full of stars, its warm lamp glowing, a lit cottage window beside it, calm moonlit sea below.
- **farm-night**: A farmhouse with one warm lit kitchen window at night, silver moonlit fields with low mist, a sleeping red barn, a starry sky.
- **rainy-bookshop**: A tiny old bookshop on a cobbled street on a rainy night, warm lamplight in the window, stacks of books, rain-shining cobblestones, a sleepy cat on the sill.
- **country-inn**: A stone country inn at the end of a snowy valley at night, warm glowing windows and smoke from the chimney, mist in the valley, a bright moon over the hills.
- **canal-boat**: A painted canal boat drifting along a still moonlit canal at night, a small glowing lantern at the bow, willow trees, a stone bridge, reflections in the water.
- **night-garden**: A walled garden at night, a winding gravel path, lavender and roses glowing in moonlight, a wooden bench, a still pond reflecting stars.
- **forest-cabin**: A small wooden cabin in a deep green forest on a rainy night, a warm glowing window, smoke from the chimney, soft rain and mist between tall pines.
- **moonlit-lake**: A small wooden rowing boat on a perfectly still mountain lake under a huge full moon, stars reflected in the water, soft mist, distant dark hills.
